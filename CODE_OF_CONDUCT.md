# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful, constructive, and professional.
- Welcome questions and differing levels of experience.
- Focus criticism on the work, not the person.
- Protect confidential, tenant, customer, and security-sensitive information.
- Use the project only in authorized synthetic environments.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disclosure of sensitive data,
or encouragement of unsafe security testing are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns through the owning organization's approved private
channel. Repository owners should replace this section with the appropriate
contact before inviting external contributors.

Maintainers may remove content or participation that violates these standards.
